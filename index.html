About Me

I am a Master's student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor. My research interests lie in developing and applying novel machine learning methods, physics-based computational models, and optimization techniques to real-world systems.

I am currently working with Prof. Raj Rao Nadakuditi on computational electromagnetics and scattering matrix methods, translating a MATLAB-based research codebase for electromagnetic wave scattering simulations into Julia and Python. I also work with Prof. Ziyou Song at the Electric Vehicle Center, where I have built physics-based electrochemical impedance models for Li-ion battery characterization using differential evolution optimization, and developed attention-enhanced sequence-to-sequence models for battery aging prediction.

Before joining UMich, I completed my B.Tech in Electrical Engineering from IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, where I developed a strong foundation in machine learning and signal processing. I am actively seeking PhD opportunities for Fall 2026 in areas spanning scientific machine learning, physics-informed modeling, and computational optimization.
